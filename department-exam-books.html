Exam Strategy: Use Books Only in the Last 30 Minutes

Books are allowed in Paper III, but searching through them takes a lot of time. If you start the paper by looking up answers, you may run out of time and leave easy questions unattempted. First attempt every question you can answer from your own preparation. Mark the doubtful ones, and open the books only in the last 30 minutes (after about 1 hour 30 minutes of the 2-hour paper) to check them.

0–90 min Attempt from memory, mark doubtful questionsLast 30 min Verify marked questions using books
